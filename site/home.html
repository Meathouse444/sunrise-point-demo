The Midcoast by season

Tulips in our gardens, quiet trails in Camden Hills State Park, the first lobster rolls of the year.

Summer

A schooner sail out of Camden Harbor, the Islesboro ferry from Lincolnville Beach, the Farnsworth in Rockland.

Fall

Foliage from the top of Mount Battie, crisp sunrises, and a fire on your deck after dinner.

Good to know

Questions before you book

Are you in Camden?

We're on the water in Lincolnville, about ten minutes north of Camden's harbor by car, so you get Camden's restaurants without the traffic.

When are you open?

From [May] through [October]. We take reservations all winter.

Can we bring children or pets?

Guests 14 and older are welcome. We aren't able to host pets.

Is there a minimum stay?

Two nights. Rates include breakfast for two; a third guest in a four-person room is $50 a night.
