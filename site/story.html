Our story

Hosted by Matt & MJ, on the point since 2021

We found the inn on a winter drive from New Hampshire, when it was closed for the season. We took over that spring and have hosted every season since: at breakfast, in the gardens and out on the lawn at dawn.

"We came here, it was closed, we drove down the drive, it was amazing."MJ Ruffing

Photo: Matt & MJ in the gardens

A day at Sunrise Point

From first light to the last log on the fire

  1. Dawn

    The sun over Islesboro

    Coffee roasted in Maine, an Adirondack chair, and lobster boats heading out.

  2. Morning

    Breakfast in the conservatory

    A full gourmet breakfast, or brought to your room.

  3. Afternoon

    Out exploring, or not

    Camden's harbor, a schooner sail, or a book on the lawn with fresh cookies.

  4. Evening

    A fire and a nightcap

    Dinner in town, then the library bar or your own fireplace.
